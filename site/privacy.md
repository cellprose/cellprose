# Privacy

Cellprose collects almost no data. The playground runs in your browser and your workbooks are never uploaded. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when a page loads, and our hosting provider keeps ordinary request logs. We set no cookies.

**Page visits.** Each page tells tablient.com, the hosted service by the same maker, its path and the site that linked to it. Only a count per day, page and linking site is kept: no cookie, no address, no device or visitor id, so one visit cannot be told apart from another or tied to you. Nothing is sent when your browser asks not to be tracked (Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control). Counts are deleted after 400 days.

**Launch news.** If you sign up, tablient.com keeps your email address, where you signed up and when you confirmed. It emails a confirmation link first and adds nothing until you use it; unconfirmed addresses are deleted after 7 days. Emails go out through Resend, a few a year, and each has a link that deletes your address from the list. See the [Tablient privacy policy](https://tablient.com/privacy).
